Consent to Participate in Research

Study Title: Review Score Challenge: Understanding the Distribution of NeurIPS Review Scores across Fine-Grained Research Topics with an Online Game and an Interactive Visualization Tool — Activity 1: Review Score Challenge Game

Principal Investigator: Haw-Shiuan Chang, College of Information Science

Consent Version: 11/08/2026

You are being asked to participate in a research study. Your participation in this research study is voluntary and you do not have to participate. This document contains important information about this study and what to expect if you decide to participate. Please consider the information carefully. Feel free to ask questions before making your decision whether to participate.

This study has two separate activities: (1) the Review Score Challenge game and (2) trying our visualization tool and answering a short questionnaire about it. Each activity has its own consent form. You may take part in either activity or both. This form is for the Review Score Challenge game.

What is the purpose of this study?

We want to learn how well researchers can guess which machine learning research topics received higher review scores, and more disagreement among reviewers, at the NeurIPS 2025 conference. The results will help us understand how review scores differ across research topics and improve our tools for exploring them.

Who can participate?

You may participate if you are 18 years of age or older.

What will I be asked to do?

  • Play the game. In each round, you will see two research categories. Each has a short title and an illustration. You will guess which category received the higher average review score and which had more disagreement among reviewers. At the end, you will see your score and a breakdown of your answers.
  • Enter the gift-card lottery (optional). After the game, you may fill out a lottery form with your name, email address, school or institution, role (for example, student or professor), research area, and any optional details.

The game shows only NeurIPS 2025 paper information and review scores that are already publicly available on OpenReview. Scores are grouped by topic, and no reviewer identities are shown. No audio, video, or photographs will be recorded.

Time commitment

The game takes about 10 minutes. You can play on your own computer or phone.

Risks and benefits

The risks of this activity are minimal and no greater than those of everyday life. You may find the game boring or frustrating. If you are a NeurIPS author, you may see grouped review scores for topics close to your own work; these scores are already public. If you enter the lottery, there is a small risk that your contact information could be disclosed. We take the steps described below to reduce this risk.

You will not benefit directly from participating in this study. You may learn something about how review scores differ across NeurIPS research topics.

Alternatives to participating

The alternative is to not participate. You may still choose to take part in the visualization tool activity only.

Payment

  • One $25 Amazon gift card will be given away by a random drawing after data collection ends.
  • For each activity, there is one drawing for the whole study. Each person may enter only once.
  • Entering the lottery is optional. You do not have to finish this activity, or take part in any activity, to enter.
  • Your chance of winning depends on how many people enter. For example, if 500 people enter, the chance of winning a gift card is 1 in 500.
  • Winners will be contacted by his/her email and the gift card will be sent electronically.

Any payment for participation in a research study is considered taxable income for you. For any compensation you receive, we are required to obtain identifiable information such as your name and email address for financial compliance purposes.

Confidentiality of information

You do not need to log in or give your name or email to play the game. Your answers will be assigned a random code number (a session ID). If you also enter the lottery, your lottery entry will be linked to your game answers through this code. Your name, email, and other lottery details will be stored in an encrypted and password-protected file that only the research team can access. They will be destroyed within 90 days after the gift cards are sent. After that, your answers can no longer be linked to you.

Your name will not be used in any report. Results will be reported only in summary form, in a way that will not identify you.

Your information may be used for future research or shared with another researcher for future research studies without additional consent. Only de-identified information (your game answers, without your name, email, or other contact details) will be used or shared in this way. For example, we may release de-identified data together with our publications.

The information that you provide in the study will be handled confidentially. However, there may be circumstances where this information must be released or shared as required by law. The University of Arizona Institutional Review Board may review the research records for monitoring purposes.

Taking part is voluntary

You may skip any round and stop at any time by closing the page, without penalty. If you would like us to remove answers you have already submitted, email us your session ID or the email address you used for the lottery. We can remove your answers only while they can still be linked to you, before your contact details are destroyed.

Agreeing to participate

Signatures are not collected for this online activity. By submitting your answer, you confirm that you are 18 years of age or older and that you have read this form. You are also agreeing to allow your responses to be used for research purposes as described above.
