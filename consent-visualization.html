Consent to Participate in Research

Study Title: Review Score Challenge: Understanding the Distribution of NeurIPS Review Scores across Fine-Grained Research Topics with an Online Game and an Interactive Visualization Tool — Activity 2: Visualization Tool Questionnaire

Principal Investigator: Haw-Shiuan Chang, College of Information Science

Consent Version: 11/08/2026

You are being asked to participate in a research study. Your participation in this research study is voluntary and you do not have to participate. This document contains important information about this study and what to expect if you decide to participate. Please consider the information carefully. Feel free to ask questions before making your decision whether to participate.

This study has two separate activities: (1) the Review Score Challenge game and (2) trying our visualization tool and answering a short questionnaire about it. Each activity has its own consent form. You may take part in either activity or both. This form is for the visualization tool and its questionnaire.

What is the purpose of this study?

We have built an interactive visualization tool that shows NeurIPS 2025 research topics and their review scores. We want to learn whether the tool helps people understand how review scores are spread across NeurIPS research subdomains, and how we can improve it.

Who can participate?

You may participate if you are 18 years of age or older.

What will I be asked to do?

  • Try the visualization tool. You will explore an interactive map of NeurIPS 2025 research topics, colored by average review score. You can search, zoom, and click topics to see their papers and scores. The tool runs in your web browser and does not send any information to the research team.
  • Answer a short questionnaire. You will rate how useful and easy to use the tool was, and whether it changed your understanding of how review scores are spread across topics. You may also write optional comments. You may skip any question. We will conduct a gift-card lottery on the responses that are not automatically generated.

It shows only NeurIPS 2025 paper information and review scores that are already publicly available on OpenReview. No reviewer identities are shown. No audio, video, or photographs will be recorded.

Time commitment

This activity takes about 10 minutes: about 5 minutes to try the tool and about 5 minutes for the questionnaire.

Risks and benefits

The risks of this activity are minimal and no greater than those of everyday life. You may find the activity boring. If you are a NeurIPS author, you may see review scores for topics and papers close to your own work; these scores are already public. If you enter the lottery, there is a small risk that your contact information could be disclosed. We take the steps described below to reduce this risk.

You will not benefit directly from participating in this study. You may learn something about how review scores differ across NeurIPS research topics.

Alternatives to participating

The alternative is to not participate. You may still choose to take part in the game activity only.

Payment

  • One $25 Amazon gift cards will be given away by a random drawing after data collection ends.
  • For each activity, there is one drawing for the whole study. Each person may enter only once.
  • Your chance of winning depends on how many people enter. For example, if 500 people enter, the chance of winning a gift card is 1 in 500.
  • Winners will be contacted by his/her email and the gift card will be sent electronically.

Any payment for participation in a research study is considered taxable income for you. For any compensation you receive, we are required to obtain identifiable information such as your name and email address for financial compliance purposes.

Confidentiality of information

The information that you give in the questionnaire will be anonymous. Your name will not be collected or linked to your answers. The lottery form is separate from the questionnaire, and your lottery entry will not be linked to your questionnaire answers.

If you write comments, it may be possible to deduce your identity from them. However, there will be no attempt to do so, and your data will be reported in a way that will not identify you.

Questionnaire answers are stored in a password-protected account that only the research team can access. Lottery details are stored in an encrypted and password-protected file and will be destroyed within 90 days after the gift cards are sent.

Your information may be used for future research or shared with another researcher for future research studies without additional consent. Only de-identified questionnaire answers will be used or shared in this way. For example, we may release de-identified data together with our publications.

The information that you provide in the study will be handled confidentially. However, there may be circumstances where this information must be released or shared as required by law. The University of Arizona Institutional Review Board may review the research records for monitoring purposes.

Agreeing to participate

Signatures are not collected for this online activity. By submitting your answer, you confirm that you are 18 years of age or older and that you have read this form. You are also agreeing to allow your responses to be used for research purposes as described above.
